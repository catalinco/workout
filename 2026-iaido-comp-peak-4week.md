## Key Changes from Base Program

1. **Reps drop, weight goes up slightly** — Shift from 5-8 rep hypertrophy range to 2-3 rep strength/power range on compounds
2. **Power Cleans appear twice per week** — The most iaido-specific lift (explosive hip extension for nukitsuke); moved to Day 3 as well
3. **Volume tapers each week** — Sets reduce progressively so you arrive fresh
4. **Isolation work removed** — No curls, lateral raises, or calf work; every exercise serves the competition
5. **Incline DB Press and Bulgarian Split Squats dropped** — Replaced by more power clean and anti-rotation work
6. **Wind Mills dropped** — Replaced by Pallof Press for direct anti-rotation carryover

## Loading Guidelines

- **W1:** Increase weight ~5-10% above cycle 2 working weights (lower reps allow this)
- **W2:** Same weight as W1, fewer sets
- **W3:** Drop weight to ~90% of W1, minimal volume — you should feel sharp, not tired
- **W4:** Light weights (~80-85%), keep sessions short and crisp. Friday is travel day — no training.

## Notes

- Prioritize sleep (8+ hours) especially in weeks 3-4
- Keep tango light in W3, skip tango in W4 if legs feel heavy
- Iaido practice should shift to competition kata polish — no new techniques
- Stay hydrated and keep nutrition consistent; this is not the time to cut weight
